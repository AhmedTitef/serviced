Your records

Where are my records kept?

On your iPhone, inside the app. Once a week Serviced also writes a plain copy you can open in the Files app, under On My iPhone › Serviced › Backups: spreadsheets of your records, cars and reminders, your receipts and photos, and the database itself.

With iCloud on for Serviced, your records also sync to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

Moving to a new iPhone

If you move with Quick Start or restore an iPhone backup, Serviced comes along with everything in it. With iCloud on for Serviced, signing in with the same Apple Account is enough.

You can also keep the weekly copies in iCloud Drive, from Records › Keep weekly copies in iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, open Records › Restore from a backup and pick the newest folder named Serviced and a date.

Getting back a deleted record

Deleted records wait 30 days in Records › Recently deleted. Swipe left on one and tap Restore.

Restoring from a backup

Open Records › Restore from a backup and pick a weekly copy folder, or the database file inside one. Before anything changes, you'll see how many cars and records the backup holds and the date of its newest record.

The records in the app now are set aside first, so you can undo the restore straight after.

Bringing records in, and passing them on

Bringing records from another app

Serviced reads exports from Vehicle Maintenance Tracker, Fuelly, Drivvo, Road Trip MPG, Simply Auto and Fuelio, and any spreadsheet saved as CSV with a date column. Records › Bring records from another app shows how to get the file out of each one.

You see what will come in before it's added, and Past imports on the same screen lets you undo an import later.

Selling your car

Open the car and tap the share button. You can send a PDF of every service and a logbook file. Both carry the same check code, so a buyer can see nothing was changed after you sent them.

A buyer who uses Serviced taps Add to my garage. Your records come with the car and stay marked as the previous owner's. The PDF and the logbook file come with the unlock; the spreadsheet and the full export are free.

The app

The one-time unlock

Serviced is free for one car, with every backup. A one-time purchase of US$9.99 unlocks the whole garage: every car, the history PDF and logbook file, unlimited receipt reading, and the widgets. Family Sharing is included.

Purchases follow your Apple Account, so the unlock usually turns on by itself on a new iPhone. If it doesn't, open the unlock screen (it appears when you add a second car) and tap Restore purchase.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are on for Serviced in the Settings app. Each car's settings choose how many days before a job is due you hear about it.

Signing out of iCloud

If you sign out of iCloud, or turn it off for Serviced, your records stay on your iPhone. They stop syncing until iCloud is back.

If a different Apple Account signs in on the iPhone, the earlier account's records are taken off it so two people's records never mix. A copy is kept, and the Records tab offers to put them back.
