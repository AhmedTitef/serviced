The short version

What we collect Nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account.
Where your records live On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if it's on. We can't see them.
What leaves your phone Only a VIN you choose to look up, and only what you choose to share.

What the app keeps, and where

Your cars, service records, photos, receipts and reminders are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. Once a week the app also writes a plain copy you can open in the Files app, under On My iPhone › Serviced. That copy lives inside the app, so deleting the app deletes it too. You can also pick a folder of your own for the weekly copy, such as one in iCloud Drive, and those copies stay there even if the app is deleted or the phone is lost.

There is no Serviced account and no Serviced server. We can't see your records, and nobody can lock you out of them.

iCloud

If iCloud is on for Serviced, your records also sync through your own private iCloud database, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account see them. Apple keeps that data under your account. We can't see it.

If you sign out of iCloud or turn it off for Serviced, your records stay on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't share data with anyone for advertising or any other purpose.

The one thing that leaves your phone

If you tap Look up next to a VIN, the app sends only the VIN to the US government's free vehicle database (NHTSA vPIC, vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) to fill in the make, model and year. Like any web request, it also reaches NHTSA with your phone's internet address. We never receive any of it. NHTSA's own privacy policy covers that request. If you don't use VIN lookup, nothing leaves your phone.

Receipt scanning reads the text with Apple's on-device tools. The image isn't uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

When you share

Serviced only sends a history PDF, a logbook file or an export when you choose to share it, to whoever you pick. The app never asks for your name or address, so they aren't in those files.

Deleting your data

Deleted records wait 30 days in Recently Deleted, then they're gone, from iCloud too if it's on. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone, including the weekly copies kept inside it. Your records in iCloud, and copies you chose to keep in a folder of your own, stay there until you delete them.

Children

Serviced isn't directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.
